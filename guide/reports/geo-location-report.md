# Geo Location Report

In AzonPress, the **Geo Location** feature in the **Reports** section allows you see where your clicks are coming from around the world. It shows a **Geo Tracking Map** and a country-wise click breakdown for your **Amazon Products**, **Comparison Tables**, and **Custom Products**.

You can also filter the report by date to see clicks for a specific time period. This article will show you how to view and understand the Geo Location Report.

## Geo Location Reports with AzonPress

>**Remember**, **to view** this **GEO Location Report**, first you **need to configure GEO Tracking with Maxmind GEO-IP API Key**. To Configure the Maxmind API Key, you can follow this [Documentation](/guide/configuring-amazon-api/geo-tracking-targeting-settings).

Go to **Reports** from the **AzonPress Navbar**, click the **Geo Location** option, and get your Geo Location Report for all your Products.

## Explanation of Geo Location Report Options

All the options available within the Geo Location reports are briefly explained below:

 * **Timeframe Filter:** Here, you can filter click reports based on timeframes such as **Year** (ongoing year), **Last Month**, **This Month** (ongoing month), **Last Week**, **Today** (ongoing day), or select **All** to show the overall report for the entire time range.
 * **Date Filter:** In this option, you can use the **Date Filter** to view reports from a specific custom timeline.
 * **Show Clicks From Each Country:** You can see exactly how many clicks you received from each individual country by clicking this button.
 * **Zoom Controls:** Use the **Plus (+)** and **Minus (-)** icons to zoom in and zoom out on your Geo Tracking Map for better visualization.

> **Remember**, geo data is also captured for **Gutenberg block** product links and **standalone Cloaked URL** visits, so this map reflects visitor locations from every link type, not just Product and Comparison Tables.
