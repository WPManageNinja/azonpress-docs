# Dashboard of AzonPress

The **Dashboard** of AzonPress displays the **Overall Click Reports** for all your affiliate products, **Affiliate Statistics**, and a **Usage Guide** to make the plugin more user-friendly. This article will guide you through the explanation of the main **Dashboard** of **AzonPress**.

## Accessing the Dashboard

To access the overview page and learn about all available options, follow these steps:

Log in to your WordPress admin panel. Navigate to **AzonPress > Dashboard** from the sidebar menu.

Alternatively, you can click the **Dashboard** tab within the **AzonPress** navigation bar.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Click Tracking Overview

The top section of the dashboard shows the **Click Tracking Overview**, which gives a quick view of how your audience interacts with your affiliate links:

 * **Total Clicks:** The cumulative number of times users have clicked on all affiliate products across your entire site.
 * **Unique Clicks:** The number of clicks received from distinct visitors, helping you distinguish between repeat clicks and new interest.
 * **Total Views:** The total number of times your affiliate products, tables, or boxes have been rendered and viewed by your visitors.
 * **Total Countries:** The geographical reach of your traffic, showing the total number of unique countries from which you received clicks.

### Click Tracking Report

This section shows a **line chart** of your total clicks over recent days, helping you track traffic changes and spot high-performance periods.

### Affiliate Statistics

This module provides a bird’s-eye view of your site's affiliate health and configuration:

 * **Product Table Count:** Displays a statistic of how many product comparison tables you have currently deployed on your site.
 * **API Status:** Shows the real-time activity status of your Amazon API Key to ensure your product data remains synchronized and active.

### View Usage Guide

Clicking the **View Usage Guide** button redirects you to the documentation section. Here, you will find necessary documentation providing proper guidelines on:

 * Creating and managing comparison tables.
 * Customizing product display boxes.
 * Setting up advanced plugin features.
