# How To Use AzonPress With Gutenberg Blocks

AzonPress offers you some interesting **Gutenberg Blocks** which will give you a lot of opportunities to show your products. This article will guide you through the process of how you can use your **Gutenberg blocks** and what your **AzonPress Gutenberg Blocks** can do.

## AzonPress Gutenberg Blocks

To learn how to use AzonPress with Gutenberg Blocks, follow the steps accordingly.

First, go to the **Page/Post** where you want to add products from your WordPress Site.

Now, click on the **Plus (+) Icon** button and search for **Azonpress** and you will get all the available **Blocks of Azonpress**. These are:

1. Azonpress Amazon Block
2. Azp Single Product
3. Azp Pros and Cons
4. Azp Call to Action
5. App Notice
6. App Notification

![All Gutenberg Blocks](/guide/public/images/getting-started/how-to-use-azonpress-with-gutenberg-blocks/all-gutenberg-blocks.webp)

**A detailed explanation of the Blocks mentioned above is given below –**

### Azonpress Amazon Block

With the **Azonpress Amazon Block**, you can create a Product Page. You can add Product Elements like the Product link, CTA, and many more. Inside this block, you will get **another five** different **tabs** for **showcasing** the **Amazon Products**. These are:

* Product Page
* Product Elements
* Best Seller List
* Comparisons Tables
* Product Tables

A brief explanation of the five tabs mentioned above is given below –

#### Product Page

Let's start with the **Product Page**. To **create** a **Product Page** just **search** for the **product.** You can **add single** or **multiple products** to your product page by selecting them.

From the **Advanced** option, you can also choose the **AzonPress Template** option for the product page according to your needs where you will get **six types** of different templates.

![Product Page under AzonPress Amazon Blocks](/guide/public/images/getting-started/how-to-use-azonpress-with-gutenberg-blocks/product-page-under-azonpress-amazon-blocks.webp)

#### Product Elements

Product Elements will give you some product Text Link, Product Image Link, CTA Link, and Feature Box.

![Product Elements under AzonPress Amazon Blocks](/guide/public/images/getting-started/how-to-use-azonpress-with-gutenberg-blocks/product-elements-under-azonpress-amazon-blocks.png.webp)

#### Bestseller List

Azonpress Amazon Block also shows you the Bestseller List. You just need to **select** the **Category** of the bestseller product. Then give the **search term** to make your search more specific.

You can also **set the limit** here on how many products you want to show on your product page. In the **Advanced** option, you will **get some templates** for your product showing.

![Bestseller list under AzonPress Amazon Blocks](/guide/public/images/getting-started/how-to-use-azonpress-with-gutenberg-blocks/bestseller-list-under-azonpress-amazon-blocks.png.webp)

#### Comparison Table

On this block, you will also get the option to **add** your **existing Comparison Table/s** just by selecting them from the drop-down menu, and then your comparison table will be added to your page.

![Comparisons Tables under AzonPress Amazon Blocks](/guide/public/images/getting-started/how-to-use-azonpress-with-gutenberg-blocks/comparisons-tables-under-azonpress-amazon-blocks.png.webp)

#### Product Tables

The last feature of this block will allow you to add your **existing Product Table/s**. Just **select** your **product table** from the drop-down menu to add to your page with this block.

![Product Tables under AzonPress Amazon Blocks](/guide/public/images/getting-started/how-to-use-azonpress-with-gutenberg-blocks/product-tables-under-azonpress-amazon-blocks.png.webp)

### Azp Single Product Block

**Azp Single Product** block **is for directly adding your product via URL**. After adding the product you **can customize** the product as you want from the block section of the Azp single product.

![Azp single product block](/guide/public/images/getting-started/how-to-use-azonpress-with-gutenberg-blocks/azp-single-product-block.webp)

Additionally, you will get the option to **add** your **Product manually** and a lot of customization options to show your product as you want.

![Azp single product drift Gif](/guide/public/images/getting-started/how-to-use-azonpress-with-gutenberg-blocks/azp-single-product-drift.webp)

### Azp Pros & Cons Block

**Azp Pros and Cons Block** is **for adding the Pros and Cons of a product.**

Additionally, you will **get a lot of settings** to **customize** your Pros and Cons features in the **Block** option for the Pro and Cons.

You **can** also **change** the **color**, **add headings**, **change typography**, **add a lot of icons**, and **many more**.

![Azp pros and cons Block](/guide/public/images/getting-started/how-to-use-azonpress-with-gutenberg-blocks/azp-pros-and-cons-block.webp)

### Azp Notice Block

With **Azp Notice Block** you **can add a notice with your product**. You **can make** a lot of **customization** for your Notice Block from the **Block** option in the right sidebar for this Notice Block.

![Azp Notice block](/guide/public/images/getting-started/how-to-use-azonpress-with-gutenberg-blocks/azp-notice-block.webp)

### Azp Button

**Azp Button** is **for adding a button** to **create a call to action** button **for** your **product**. **Customize** this button from the right sidebar **Block** option.

![Azp Button](/guide/public/images/getting-started/how-to-use-azonpress-with-gutenberg-blocks/azp-button.webp)

### Azp Star Rating

If you want to **add any rating against your product** you can simply add this block on your page to add the rating section.

![Azp star rating](/guide/public/images/getting-started/how-to-use-azonpress-with-gutenberg-blocks/azp-star-rating.webp)
